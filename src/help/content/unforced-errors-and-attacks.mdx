Entering two shots a point is enough to think about. So the other things worth knowing about a point, such as whether a miss was unforced or who attacked first, get their own pass afterwards. That pass is called a **sweep**: it goes back through the match one point at a time and asks one question about each. It's quicker and more accurate than ticking while you enter, because you're only watching for one thing.

## The four sweeps

### {SWEEPS.unforced.name}

**Covers:** {SWEEPS.unforced.scope.toLowerCase()}. {SWEEPS.unforced.instruction}

### {SWEEPS.early_attack.name}

**Covers:** {SWEEPS.early_attack.scope.toLowerCase()}. {SWEEPS.early_attack.instruction}

### {SWEEPS.attacked_first.name}

**Covers:** {SWEEPS.attacked_first.scope.toLowerCase()}, except those ticked as attacked straight away. {SWEEPS.attacked_first.instruction}

### {SWEEPS.lucky.name}

**Covers:** {SWEEPS.lucky.scope.toLowerCase()}. {SWEEPS.lucky.instruction}

A few points are left out because the shots already answer the question. A service fault is never in a sweep. Attacked straight away skips points decided by the serve or the receive: there, the receive was the losing or winning shot, so whether it was an attack is already known.

If you're doing both attack sweeps, do **attacked straight away** first. Every point ticked there counts as attacking first too, so the attacked first sweep has fewer points to go through.

## Run a sweep

1. Open the match and choose **Edit points and sweeps**. It's on the match list, at the bottom of the analysis page and on the match complete screen.
2. Under **Sweeps**, choose **Start sweep** on the one you want. Open the video alongside it.
3. Each point shows the score before it was played, which is what the video shows as the rally starts, who served, and the two shots you entered.
4. Answer **Yes** or **No, next point**. No means no, so you only need to act on the points that count. The Yes button says what it means, such as "Unforced error", or "Third-ball attack" on your serve and "Attacked their serve" on theirs.
5. Made a mistake? **Back a point** goes back one. You can also pick any point in the list beside the question to jump to it.

The list beside the question shows the set's points with their scores. If the score there doesn't match the video, a point has been missed. That's the moment to check before carrying on.

Every answer saves as you go. **Finish later** takes you back to the points editor, which shows where you stopped, and **Carry on** picks up from there.

On a phone the instructions fold away under **What counts**, and the answer buttons stay at the bottom of the screen. On a laptop, <kbd>Y</kbd> means yes, <kbd>N</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> means no, and <kbd>Backspace</kbd> goes back a point.

## When a tag starts counting

Until a sweep is finished, an unticked point could mean "no" or "not looked at yet". So a tag only reaches the analysis once it has at least one tick or has been confirmed, and the analysis page treats unconfirmed counts as "in the points you marked".

A tag is confirmed when:

- you finish its sweep, or
- you choose **That's all of them**, on the points editor or on the analysis page.

If you ticked a few while entering and know there are no more, **That's all of them** confirms them without a sweep. The analysis page asks you about this whenever a tag has ticks but isn't confirmed. Only you see that question: it never appears on a share link or in a PDF.

Lucky shots never ask, because they don't change anything but the lucky count.

Sweeps you don't use stay off the analysis page. A match with no attacked first ticks has no Who attacked first section.

## Ticking by hand

The points editor also has a column of ticks for every point: **Unforced**, **Attacked first**, **Straight away** and **Lucky**. Each tick saves as you make it. Ticking by hand doesn't confirm the tag, because the app can't know you've looked at every point. See [Fix a point entered wrongly](/help/fix-a-point).

## Only your own attacks are ticked

Attacked first is one tick, for the player. When it's not ticked, the app looks at the last two shots: if either was an attacking shot, the opponent attacked first. If neither was, the point is "unclear". Nobody attacked, or someone attacked earlier in the rally and it ended with a push or a block. Unclear points are shown on their own and never counted as the opponent's. See [Attacked first](/help/glossary#attacked-first).
