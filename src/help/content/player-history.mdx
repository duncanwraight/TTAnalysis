A player's history adds up every match entered for them, so you can see what's changing and which opponents cause trouble. Open it with **History** on the player's card on the Players page, or with the link on a match's details card.

It counts the points from every match together, rather than averaging each match's percentages. A match of 90 points counts for more than a match of 30.

## Filters

- **3 months, 6 months, 12 months** or **All**, which is where it starts.
- **Finished matches only**, on by default. Untick it to include matches still being entered.
- **Event**, when the player's matches have one, to look at a single league or tournament.

The filters apply to all three tabs.

## Overview

**Totals:** matches won and lost, sets, and the share of points won overall, on serve and on receive.

### What stands out

At most three things, from:

- [sets v points](#sets-v-points), when it's well away from zero,
- the biggest move in a trend,
- a shot used in 4 or more matches that lost more points than it won in 60% or more of them,
- the hardest kind of opponent, by style, hand or rubbers, when the player's share of sets against them is 8 points or more below their usual.

Each one links to the trend or tab it comes from. Groups of fewer than three matches are never used.

### Over time

Seven charts, one dot per match, oldest on the left:

- sets won, points won, points won on serve, points won on receive,
- close finishes won from 8-all,
- unforced errors per 10 points,
- attacked first.

The two lines compare the last 5 counted matches with the 5 before them (half and half with fewer than 10). There's no comparison until the player has 6 counted matches.

Unforced errors and attacked first only count matches where that tag is [confirmed](/help/glossary#confirmed), and close finishes only matches that reached 8-all. A match that doesn't count shows as a grey dot on the bottom line, so it's clear what's missing.

### Matches

Every match in the filters, newest first, with links to each analysis.

## Shots

Every shot across the matches, for the player or for their opponents. **Won** counts the points the shot won, and **Lost** the points it lost, whether it missed or set up the winner. The strip beside each shot shows the net in each match: a shot that loses every time stands apart from one that had one bad match. Shots used only a few times are folded away underneath.

Unforced errors only count matches where they're confirmed, and the page says how many that is.

## Opponents

The matches grouped by the opponent's **rubbers**, **style of play** or **hand**, as they were set on each match. Each group shows matches won and lost, then sets, points, serve, receive and close finishes. Sets come first, because sets win matches.

The small number after each figure is the difference from the player's figure across all matches. Choose a group to list its matches.

- Groups of fewer than three matches are greyed out and marked **Too few**: too few to read anything into.
- A match against two kinds of rubber, such as long pips and anti, counts in both.
- Matches with nothing set for the opponent are grouped together.

### Sets v points

The share of sets won minus the share of points won.

- **Well below zero:** the player won plenty of points but not the sets. Something goes at the end of sets, such as a stroke under pressure, or nerves. Close finishes and the end of sets on the video are the place to look.
- **Well above zero:** the opposite. The player won the sets that mattered.

## Sharing
