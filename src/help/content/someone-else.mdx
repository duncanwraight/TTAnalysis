You might coach someone and want to go through their matches with them, or you might have filmed a friend at a league match. Either way, the match goes on your account, under their name. They don't need an account of their own.

## Players

**Players** in the header lists you and everyone whose matches you enter. Each player has:

- a **style of play**, **hand** and the **rubber** on each side, used to fill in new matches,
- **notes**, which only you see,
- a **History** button, once they have a match. See [Player history](/help/player-history).

**Add a player** adds someone before their first match. A new match can also add them as you go: pick **Someone new** under Player and type their name. Renaming a player renames their matches too.

Opponents aren't listed. Their style is set on each match, and the next match against the same name starts with it filled in.

## "You" on the analysis page

The analysis page and the coach's summary always speak to the player the match is about. On a friend's match, "you" means your friend, and "your serve" means theirs. The heading names them, such as "Sam Carter vs Alex Morgan", so a share link makes sense to whoever opens it.

## Which player is you

One player can be marked as you. New matches start with your name filled in, and your own older matches, entered before players existed, are linked to you.

The first time you create a match, tick **This is me** only if the name you typed is yours. It's unticked by default, so entering a friend's match first doesn't make them you.

If the wrong player is marked as you, open your own player on the Players page, tick **This is me** and save. The old one becomes an ordinary player and keeps their matches.

## Styles, hand and rubbers

Each side of a match can have:

- **Style of play**, such as Two-winged looper or Classical defender, from a list kept by the app. Pick the nearest. A short description shows under the list when you pick one.
- **Hand:** right, left or not known.
- **Forehand and backhand rubber:** inverted, short pips, medium pips, long pips, anti, or not known.

All of them are optional. The coach's summary uses them for tactics, and the player's history can group matches by the opponent's style, hand and rubbers.

A new match copies the player's style from their page. Changing it in the [points editor](/help/fix-a-point#change-the-match-details) changes that match only, and changing the player's page later doesn't rewrite matches already entered. A player's style can change over the years, and old matches keep the style they were played with.

## Notes are private

A player's notes are for you: what they're working on, what tends to go wrong, anything a coach would want in mind. They can be up to 1,000 characters.

- Nobody else sees them. Share links never show them.
- The coach's summary reads them, and checks what they mention against the counts, such as how often a shot you named was played.
- The summary never quotes them or says they exist. The player reads the summary and usually didn't write the notes, so a note comes through as a neutral observation about their game, followed by what the match showed.

Changing a player's notes marks their coach's summaries as out of date, so they can be rewritten with the new notes.

## Deleting a player

Edit the player and choose **Delete player**. Their matches stay, with their name, but lose the link to the player and the player's notes. You can't delete the player marked as you.
