Matches are private until you share them. Sharing makes a link that anyone can open without an account: a coach, a teammate, or the player whose match you entered. You can share one match, or everything for one player.

## Share a match

1. Open the match's analysis.
2. Choose **Share analysis** at the bottom of the page. The link is made and copied, and the button reads **Link copied**. If your browser doesn't allow copying, the link appears in a box to copy by hand.
3. Paste it wherever you like.

Choosing **Share analysis** again gives the same link.

## Share a player's history

1. Open **Players** and choose **History** on the player.
2. Choose **Share**, then **Create a link**, then **Copy**.

The link shows the player's history and opens each of their matches from it, including matches you enter later. You don't need to share each match as well. The button reads **Shared** while the link works.

## What the person opening the link sees

- The analysis page, read-only, with the match details, both players' styles and the video link if there is one.
- The coach's summary tab, if the match has a summary.
- For a history: the Overview, Shots and Opponents tabs, the filters, and every match's analysis and summary.
- **Export to PDF**, so they can keep a copy.

## What they never see

- The player's notes.
- Notes you wrote to correct a coach's summary.
- Whether a summary is out of date.
- The questions and buttons meant for you, such as **That's all of them**, **Edit points and sweeps** and the share buttons.
- Your other matches and players.

## Stop sharing

- **A match:** choose **Stop sharing** at the bottom of its analysis.
- **A history:** choose **Shared**, then **Stop sharing**.

The link stops working straight away, for everyone who has it. Sharing again makes a new link, and the old one stays dead.

Stopping a history's link doesn't stop links you made to single matches, and the other way round.
