This page takes you from a match on video to its analysis. Each step links to a page with more detail, so you can skip what you already know.

## Film the match

Any phone will do. You go through the video afterwards, at home, on a phone or a laptop, so nothing needs doing courtside.

When you play it back, 1.25x or 1.5x speed is usually fast enough to follow every point. If the video is online, save its link with the match and the tracker and the analysis page can open it for you.

## Create the match

Choose **New match** and fill in:

- **Player.** Whose match it is. The first time, type your name and tick **This is me**. If you're entering a friend's match, pick them or choose **Someone new**. See [Enter a match for someone else](/help/someone-else).
- **Opponent** and **Date**. Event is optional, but it lets you filter a player's history by league or tournament later.
- **Styles.** Optional. The style of play, hand and rubbers for each side. The coach's summary uses them for tactics.
- **Best of** and **First server.** Check both against the video. They can't be changed once you start.
- **Video link** and **Notes.** Optional.

Then choose **Start entering points**.

## Enter the points

For each point you enter three things:

1. **Who won.** Tap their side of the score.
2. **The winning shot.** The last shot of the player who won the point.
3. **The losing shot.** The last shot of the player who lost it: either the shot that set up the winner, or the one that missed.

The point saves as soon as you pick the losing shot. You don't enter the rally in between, and [there's a reason for that](/help/two-shots).

If a losing shot was a miss you'd expect to make, tick **Unforced error** before you pick it. If the winner clipped the net or the edge, tick **Lucky**. Both can also be done later in one go, which is often easier.

Sets and the match finish on their own when someone gets there. See [Enter points in the tracker](/help/tracker) for Undo, End set and what to do when the video and the score drift apart.

## Mark unforced errors and attacks

Once the points are in, you can go back through the match and answer one question about each point, such as "was this an unforced error?" or "did you attack first?". Each one is called a sweep. They're optional, but they add the Unforced errors and Who attacked first sections to the analysis. See [Mark unforced errors and attacks](/help/unforced-errors-and-attacks).

## Read the analysis

Choose **See the analysis** when the match ends, or **Analysis** on the match list. The page starts with the headline numbers and a few main findings, then goes section by section: serve and receive, your winning and losing shots, unforced errors, patterns, who attacked first, shot balance, how each set went, and the opponent's shots.

Every number comes from the two shots a point you entered. Each section has a **How this works** link that explains what it counts. See [The analysis page, section by section](/help/analysis-page).
