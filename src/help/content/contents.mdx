### How does the app know who served?

You pick the first server when you create the match, and the app works out every serve after that from the rules: the serve changes every two points, every point from 10-all, and the other player serves first in the next set. Nothing else is entered. If the first server is wrong, every point's server is wrong, and so are the serve and receive figures. Check it on the video before you start, because it can't be changed afterwards.

### Can I change best of or the first server once I've started?

No. Both decide how the score and the serve run, so they're fixed once the match is created. If one is wrong, delete the match and create it again. You can change the opponent, date, event, video link and styles at any time in the [points editor](/help/fix-a-point#change-the-match-details).

### I picked the wrong winner a few points ago

If it's in the set you're entering, you can undo back to it, but Undo removes every point after it too. It's usually quicker to carry on and fix it afterwards in the points editor. See [Fix a point entered wrongly](/help/fix-a-point).

### Why does the analysis ask whether all my unforced errors are ticked?

A point with no tick could mean "not unforced" or "not looked at yet". Until you say the ticks are complete, by finishing a sweep or choosing **That's all of them**, the app can't tell the difference. See [When a tag starts counting](/help/unforced-errors-and-attacks#when-a-tag-starts-counting).

### What counts as an attacking shot?

Any shot with the attack role: loops, flicks, smashes and the like, including a flick on the receive. Every shot has a role. See [Shot roles](/help/glossary#shot-roles).

### Can the person I share with see my notes?

No. A share link never shows a player's notes or the notes you wrote to correct a coach's summary. See [Share a match or a player's history](/help/sharing).

### How do I export a PDF?

Choose **Export to PDF** at the bottom of the analysis page. It prints whichever tab is open, so open the Coach's summary tab first if that's the one you want. Buttons, links and notes only you can see are left out.

### How long does a match take to enter?

A match filmed uncut usually runs about 20 minutes, and at 1.5x speed most take around 15 minutes to enter. A video trimmed between points is often half that.

### Can I enter points on a phone?

Yes. The tracker works on a phone and on a laptop. On a wider screen you can also use the [keyboard shortcuts](/help/keyboard-shortcuts).

### Do I get the coach's summary?

It's switched on account by account, by invitation, while it's being tried out. Everything else is open to every account. See [How the coach's summary is written](/help/coach-summary).
