Every match has a points editor. Open it with **Edit points and sweeps** on the match list, at the bottom of the analysis page, or on the match complete screen. It lists every point with its score, server, winner and both shots.

Use **All sets** or a set number to narrow the list, and **Only points you lost** when you're looking for unforced errors.

## Change a tick

The four tick columns, **Unforced**, **Attacked first**, **Straight away** and **Lucky**, save as soon as you tick or untick. A few can't be ticked:

- A service fault always counts as unforced, so its tick is fixed.
- A point ticked as attacked straight away is always an attack first, so that tick is fixed while Straight away is ticked.
- Straight away only applies to points that went past the receive.

The unforced tick applies to whoever lost the point, but only the player's own unforced errors count in the analysis.

## Change a shot or the winner

Choose **Edit** on the point. The dialog shows the score before the point and who was serving, then:

- **Who won the point.** Changing it clears both shots and the unforced tick, as the tracker does, because the old shots belonged to the other player.
- **Lucky** and **Unforced error.**
- **The winning and losing shots**, opening on the point's own shots.

Choose **Save** once both shots are picked. The analysis and the points editor update straight away.

## When a winner change doesn't add up

Changing who won one point moves the score of every point after it in the set. In a set that reached 11, one change on its own never adds up: the set would end a point early, or not end at all. The usual cause is two points entered the wrong way round, so the fix is two changes.

When a change doesn't add up, the dialog says why and offers **Keep and edit another point**. Choose it, then edit the other point. Until the match adds up again:

- the changes are held, not saved, and the table shows them highlighted as if they were,
- a box above the table lists what still doesn't add up,
- the ticks are locked.

Once it adds up, choose **Save changes**. **Discard** throws the held changes away.

## What can't be changed

The points editor refuses a change that would:

- finish a set before its last point,
- leave a finished set unfinished, level, or won by the other player,
- finish the match before its last set,
- give a serve or a service fault to the player who didn't serve,
- make a service fault a losing shot.

So you can't change who won a set, and you can't add or remove a point. A change in the set still being entered can finish that set, or the match, as it would in the tracker. Best of and the first server can't be changed once the match has started. If one of them is wrong, delete the match and enter it again.

## Change the match details

The **Match details** form in the points editor changes the player, opponent, date, event, video link and both players' styles. The styles here are the ones for this match only. Changing them doesn't change the player's own page, and changing the player's page doesn't change matches already entered.

## The coach's summary after an edit

If the match has a coach's summary, editing a point or the styles marks it as out of date. The owner sees **Update the summary** on its tab. See [How the coach's summary is written](/help/coach-summary).
