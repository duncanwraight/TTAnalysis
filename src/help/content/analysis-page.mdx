Every number on the analysis page comes from the points you entered: who won each one, the winning shot and the losing shot, and any ticks. Rally shots aren't entered, so the page only ever talks about winning and losing shots, never shots played. See [Why you only enter two shots a point](/help/two-shots).

"You" is always the player the match is about, and blue is a point to them, orange a point to the opponent. Sections with nothing to show are left out.

## Match details

The result, date, event, format and video link, and each side's style, hand and rubbers if they were set. On your own view, a link opens all of that player's matches in their [history](/help/player-history).

## Headline numbers

- **Points won**, out of every point played.
- **On your serve** and **On their serve:** points won while serving and while receiving.
- **From 8-all:** points won in [close finishes](/help/glossary#close-finishes), with both players on 8 or more.
- **Your unforced errors**, out of the points you lost, once they've been ticked.

## Main findings

Up to six cards, picked by fixed rules, so a card only appears when the numbers clear the bar.

- **Match story:** a set lost from a lead of 3 or more (or 2 with the leader on 8 or more), or won from that far behind. Winning clearly more points when you attacked first (5 or more points each way, and a gap of 25 percentage points). Attacking shots dropping off in a later set. Winning fewer or more points from the 16th point of sets on. One side doing more attacking or defending. A shot the opponent kept using that you never did.
- **Work on:** a shot with 3 or more unforced errors, unforced errors bunching from 8-all, or a shot that was your losing shot 4 or more times and more often than your winning shot.
- **Scouting:** the opponent's shot that won them 5 or more points, and their shot that lost 4 or more points, three times as often as it won.

## Serve and receive

The serve and the receive are the only shots in a rally the data can pick out exactly. When the serve is the winning shot, the receive missed or wasn't returned. When the serve is the server's losing shot, the receive won the point. So for each player's serve, the section counts:

- points the serve won outright,
- points the receive won, and with which shots,
- service faults,
- **longer rallies**, the points that went past the receive, and how many you won.

If [attacked straight away](/help/glossary#attacked-straight-away) has been ticked, **Attacking straight away** shows how often you attacked the third ball after your serve, how often you attacked their serve, and how those points went. Receives that won or lost the point count without a tick, because the entered shots already show them.

## Your winning and losing shots

Each shot and hand you played as a winning or losing shot, most costly first. **Won** counts the points it won, **Lost** the points it lost, and **Net** the difference. **Unforced** counts the losing shots ticked as unforced, once unforced errors have been ticked. A service fault counts as your losing shot. Shots that only ever won are listed underneath.

The two cards below add up every forehand and every backhand, leaving out serves.

## Unforced errors

Only shown once unforced errors have been [ticked or confirmed](/help/unforced-errors-and-attacks#when-a-tag-starts-counting). Until then it offers the points editor. The definition is in the [glossary](/help/glossary#unforced-error).

- **Unforced errors** and **Forced:** how the points you lost split. Forced means the opponent won the point.
- **From 8-all:** unforced errors in close finishes.
- **By stage of the set** and **Serving and receiving:** when and where they happened.

## Patterns

A winning shot and the losing shot it was played against, when the same pair happened three times or more. For example, "forehand loop past a backhand push" five times. One list for how you won points, one for how they did. Unforced errors are left out, because the winning shot didn't cause them.

## Who attacked first

Only shown once the [attacked first sweep](/help/unforced-errors-and-attacks) has ticks. It splits the points by who served, then:

- **You attacked first:** the ticked points.
- **They attacked first:** unticked points where one of the last two shots was an attack.
- **No attack in the last two shots:** [unclear](/help/glossary#unclear) points. Never counted as the opponent's.

Each row shows how many of those points you won. Until the tag is confirmed, the counts cover the points you marked, and the section says some may be missing.

## Shot balance

Your winning and losing shots against the opponent's, grouped by [shot role](/help/glossary#shot-roles): attack, control and defence. It shows each side's share of shots in each role, how each role went, and a chart of every shot with you on the left and the opponent on the right.

**What stands out** lists at most three things, in this order:

1. a role whose share differs by 20 points or more, when both sides had 10 or more shots,
2. a shot the opponent had 5 or more times that you never had,
3. a shot one side had at least one and a half times as often, with 10 or more,
4. a shot you had 5 or more times that they never had.

The comparison is always with the opponent in the same match, never with other players.

## Match progression

One chart per set, showing the gap in the score after every point. Above the line, you were ahead. Under each chart, how many points you won on each serve in that set.

- **Points by stage of the set:** points 1 to 6, 7 to 15, 16 on, and from 8-all. The stages count both players' points, so "points 1 to 6" covers 3-3, 5-1 and 6-0 alike.
- **Longest runs:** runs of three or more points in a row, with the score they started from.
- **Lucky points:** net and edge winners for each player.

## Scouting the opponent

The opponent's winning and losing shots, most used first. A losing shot here is a point to you. Shots they used only once are listed underneath. The card beside it adds up their forehand and backhand, leaving out serves.
